**Date:** 2026-09-09

**Status:** OPEN

## Event

DEV-CBD-001 is adopted as Phase 9's Work A. The phase starts from the existing
application-component-composition CML requirement model and the agreed
usecase-driven modeling direction. The detailed V1 working boundary is recorded
in `docs/notes/usecase-driven-component-composition-v1.md` so that exploratory
reasoning remains outside normative design and specification layers.

## Recorded V1 direction

CBD Support owns attributable evidence admission and deterministic coverage
accounting. Semantic and AI providers may supply attributable interpretations,
candidates, and proposals. A human decision is required to select an existing
component or promote a proposed component. The phase must preserve explicit
alternatives, gaps, unresolved evidence, and the no-hidden-winner boundary.

The initial composition plan is transient and read-only. It does not generate,
assemble, build, publish, execute, or persist an application plan. Durable
review, approval, continuation, and internal-model storage remain DEV-CBD-002
work and are not entered by Phase 9.
