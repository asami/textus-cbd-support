# Phase 9 Integration: Composition and Dashboard

**Date:** 2026-09-09
**Phase:** Phase 9
**Status:** OPEN planning decision

## Decision

Phase 9 integrates the application-component composition direction with the
upstream Component Dashboard plan. They are complementary surfaces over
admitted evidence, not alternative product definitions.

Composition starts from application intent and preserves required capabilities,
component evidence, alternatives, gaps, proposals, provider limitations, and
explicit human decisions. Dashboard starts from one exact Component and
projects its admitted content, usage, operation, and canonical Review
condition. A composition candidate may link to Dashboard by stable component
identity, but it remains a candidate until an explicit human decision records
selection; Dashboard never turns it into a catalog fact.

## Recorded boundary

The integrated Phase keeps source attribution, authorization, redaction,
absence, and no-hidden-winner behavior common to both surfaces. It does not
authorize CML-source parsing, heuristic semantic reconstruction, a local
lifecycle engine, application generation/execution, or durable composition
plan and approval storage. The latter remains DEV-CBD-002 work.
